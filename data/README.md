# Data

All data here is synthetic, for the fictional skincare shop "Lumi Skin". None of it comes from a real company, real customers or real ad accounts.

| File | What it is | How it was made |
|---|---|---|
| `products.csv` | 40 products (id, name, category, skin type, key ingredients, price in AED, stock) | Copied unchanged from the shared synthetic Lumi Skin data, generated by `generate.py` (seed 42) |
| `brand.json` | Brand voice, language notes and brand-banned words (EN/AR/FR) | Written for this project |
| `briefs/*.json` | 30 product briefs: the first 6 in-stock products of each of the 5 categories | `python data/make_briefs.py` (deterministic templates, no randomness) |
| `campaign.csv` | 180 rows: 30 days (2026-09-08 to 2026-10-07) × 6 ad sets, with spend, impressions, clicks, purchases and revenue in AED | `python data/make_campaign.py` (NumPy seed 42) |

## Brief fields

`id`, `product_id`, `product`, `category`, `skin_type`, `key_ingredients`, `price_aed`, `audience`, `key_message`, `offer` (or null), `tone`, `placement` (facebook_feed / instagram_feed / stories / reels), `languages`, `banned_words` (extra, per language), `required_disclaimers` (per language).

- Products with strong actives (retinol, salicylic, glycolic, azelaic, lactic acid) get the line "Patch test first."
- Sunscreens get "Reapply every 2 hours." and extra banned words such as "waterproof".

## Campaign design

The six ad sets were designed to behave differently, so the analysis has something to find. The suggested actions in brackets come from `budget.py`.

- `vitc-ar-stories`: clearly good (SCALE)
- `vitc-en-igfeed`: good (SCALE)
- `spf-en-fbfeed-broad`: clearly bad, with a broad audience (PAUSE)
- `nightcream-fr-retarget`: good at first, then the click rate falls from day 15 (ad fatigue, KEEP and refresh the creative)
- `cleanser-ar-fbfeed-lal`: below break-even (CUT)
- `eyepatch-en-reels-test`: a tiny test budget with too few purchases to judge (HOLD)
